# Privacy Policy for ESL English

_Last updated: 20 October 2026_

This policy explains what information the Android app **ESL English**
(package `com.hadoso.android.storyenglish`, "the app") collects, why, and what
choices you have. The app is published by HaDoSolution ("we").

## Summary

- The app has no accounts and no sign-up. We do not ask for your name, email,
  phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
- Stories, your reading settings, your progress and any audio you download are
  stored only on your device.
- The app shows ads through Google AdMob, measures app usage through Google
  Analytics for Firebase, and reports crashes through Firebase Crashlytics.
  These Google services collect some device information, described below.

## Information stored only on your device

The following stays on your device and is never sent to us:

- Your reading speed, text size and list filter.
- The story you were reading last and the stories you marked as learned.
- Audio files you choose to download for offline listening.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all
of this.

## Information collected by third-party services

### Advertising — Google AdMob

The app is free and shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and
measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares
with Google:

- **Device and advertising identifiers**, such as the Android advertising ID
  and app set ID.
- **IP address**, which can be used to estimate your general (not precise)
  location.
- **Interactions with ads and the app**, such as app launches and ad taps.
- **Diagnostic information**, such as app performance data.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland,
the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are
loaded, and you can change your choice at any time from **Privacy settings** in
the app menu. In every region you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or
opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Android settings.

How Google uses this data: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>

### Usage analytics — Google Analytics for Firebase

To understand how the app is used and improve it, Google Analytics for Firebase
collects app-instance and advertising identifiers, a coarse location derived
from a masked IP address, and app usage events such as sessions and screen
views. This data is not linked to your name or any account.

Google's privacy policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>

### Crash reports — Firebase Crashlytics

If the app crashes or stops responding, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash
report so we can fix the problem. A report contains the technical details of
the error (such as the stack trace), the app version, the device model and
Android version, and an installation identifier. It does not contain your
stories, settings or any personal content.

Firebase privacy information: <https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy>

### Story audio

When you play or download a story's audio, the app requests the audio file from
our file server. Like any web server, it may log your IP address and the time of
the request for security and operation. We do not combine these logs with any
other data.

## How data is protected

All data the app and these services send over the internet is encrypted in
transit using HTTPS/TLS.

## We do not sell your data

We do not sell personal information. We share data only with the Google
services named above, for the purposes described.

## Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children
under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under
13. If you believe a child has used the app and you want related data removed,
contact us at the address below.

## Your choices and rights

- Reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in
  Android settings.
- In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, change your ad consent from **Privacy
  settings** in the app.
- Delete everything the app stores on your device by uninstalling it.
- Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access or delete
  personal data. Because we do not hold an account or profile for you, requests
  about ad and analytics data are best made to Google; you can also contact us
  and we will help.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date
above.
