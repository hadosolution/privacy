# Privacy Policy — Music Timer

**Last updated: 23 August 2026**

App: **Music Timer** (Canh giờ tắt nhạc), package `com.hadoso.musictimer`.
Contact: **hadosolution@gmail.com**

## Summary

The app has no accounts and no sign-in, and it does not collect your name, email
address, phone number, contacts, photos, or files. Every timer setting is stored
**on your device** and is never sent anywhere.

The app does show ads and does collect crash reports and anonymous usage counts.
Each of those is described below.

## Data kept on your device only

The following lives in the app's private storage on your phone:

- The stop time you set and when the current run started
- The length of each of the four preset buttons
- Your fade-out length choice
- The nightly schedule (on/off, time, duration)
- Your language choice
- How many timers have run to completion, used to decide when to ask you for a
  review

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

## Data that is sent off the device

### Advertising — Google AdMob

The app shows one banner at the bottom of the main screen. Google AdMob may
collect your advertising ID, device information, approximate location derived
from your IP address, and your interactions with ads, in order to select and
measure advertising.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland,
the app shows a consent form before serving personalised ads, and you can change
that choice at any time under **Settings → Privacy options**.

Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

### Crash reports — Firebase Crashlytics

When the app crashes, a report is sent containing the error type, the line where
it happened, your device model, Android version, app version, and a random
installation identifier. It contains none of your personal content.

### Usage counts — Firebase Analytics

The app records two anonymous events:

- **Timer started** — with whether you used a preset or picked a clock time, and
  where you started it from (in-app, widget, Quick Settings tile, shortcut,
  assistant, or the nightly schedule)
- **Timer completed** — when the music was actually stopped

These exist only to show which features are worth further work. Nothing
identifying you is attached to them.

Google's policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

## Permissions the app asks for, and why

| Permission | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| Exact alarms | Stopping the music at the time you set, even while the phone sleeps |
| Notifications | Showing the stop time, the one-minute warning, and the "+10 min" button |
| Foreground service (media playback) | Keeping the stop sequence alive long enough to fade the volume and stop playback |
| Modify audio settings | Fading the media volume down and restoring it |
| Receive boot completed | Re-arming your timer and schedule after a restart |
| Internet | Loading ads and sending crash reports |

The app does **not** ask for location, contacts, microphone, camera, or storage.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data
from children.

## Your choices

- Change your personalised-ads choice: **Settings → Privacy options**
  (for users in the EEA/UK/Switzerland)
- Reset or delete your advertising ID: **Android Settings → Privacy → Ads**
- Delete all app data: uninstall the app
- Request deletion of data already sent: email **hadosolution@gmail.com**

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with
a new date.
