# Video English – Privacy Policy

*Effective date: 23 October 2026*

This policy explains how the Android app **Video English** (package
`com.hadoso.videoenglish`, published by Hadoso Team) handles information.
Vietnamese summary below.

## What the app does not collect

The app has no accounts and no sign-in. It does not ask for your name, email,
contacts, location, photos, files, microphone or camera. Hadoso Team does not
run its own server for this app and does not sell personal data.

## Third-party services that process data

The app uses the following Google services. They may collect device and usage
data under their own policies:

- **Google AdMob** shows banner ads. It may collect the advertising ID, IP
  address, device information, and ad interaction data to serve, measure and
  limit ads, and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app
  asks for your consent through Google's consent message before ads are
  requested; you can change your choice at any time from the menu item
  *Privacy settings*.
- **Google Analytics for Firebase** collects anonymous usage statistics (app
  opens, screens viewed, crashes, device model, OS version, app version,
  approximate country) to help us improve the app.
- **YouTube API Services** play videos through the official YouTube embedded
  player. When you play a video, YouTube receives information as described in
  the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). By using the
  video player you agree to those terms.

Google's handling of this data is described in the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
[How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Your choices

- Reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in
  Android *Settings → Google → Ads* (or *Settings → Privacy → Ads*).
- Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland can review or withdraw ad consent from
  the app menu *Privacy settings*.
- Uninstalling the app stops all further collection by the app.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
personal information from them.

## Security and retention

Data collected by Google services is transmitted over encrypted connections and
kept according to Google's retention policies. The app itself stores nothing
about you on the device beyond what Android and these SDKs need to operate.

## Changes

We will post any change to this policy on this page and update the effective
date above.
